# Components

Every component of the library, by part: what it is, its props, its keys, and examples, each its picture and the code that drew it.

- [Layout](layout.md): Where components go on the page: stacked in rows, side by side in columns, in a grid, at a fixed place, or over one another; framed in panels, divided by rules, and reached in two presses by jump labels.
- [The frame](frame.md): Every page's frame, as Ceefax laid it out: a title band in double height, the body, a hint of what the keys do, and the four Fastext prompts in red, green, yellow and cyan. Its rows are components of their own, with a status bar and lists of keys beside them.
- [Text](text.md): Words on the page: a line in a colour, wrapped paragraphs and runs of mixed styles, the teletext sizes and effects, and the pieces of a news service, from headlines and bylines to badges, tickers and large letters.
- [Navigation](navigation.md): The ways about a channel: tabs, menus and trees to choose where to go, sections that open and shut, the way back up, the pages of a run, and a finder that goes to a page by its name.
- [Lists](lists.md): Lists to choose from, by the arrows, a digit, a coloured key or what is typed; tables to read and sort; a log that follows what happens; and the rows and scroll bar every list is built from.
- [Input](input.md): The controls a viewer sets with a remote or a keyboard: fields to type in, by a keyboard, by digits to a template or by a phone's keypad; buttons and actions; boxes, radios and switches; numbers, sliders and selects.
- [Feedback](feedback.md): What tells the viewer how things stand: how far along or how full, what is still loading, notices and toasts, a dashboard's figures, and a player for sound, with its queue, its modes and a spectrum.
- [Overlays](overlay.md): What stands over a page: a dialog that keeps the keys until it is answered, a modal that lays one over a page and dims it, and a dialog that asks for a name before it destroys anything.
- [Charts](charts.md): Figures drawn in mosaics: bars and columns scaled to the largest, lines and points on axes, heat maps, the shares of a whole as a bar or a pie, a cup's bracket and a sound's spectrum. Each says its figures in words or marks as well, so none is read by colour alone.
- [Time](time.md): Dates and times as the pages write them, always in UTC, and the components that keep time: a clock, a countdown and a stopwatch in large digits, and a calendar to choose a day in.
- [Graphics](graphics.md): Pictures in mosaic sixels: icons, bitmaps of your own, pictures and QR codes made by the engine's tools, a map with its places marked, a canvas to paint on, and things that move.
- [Documents](documents.md): Pages of several sub-pages, each named in its title band: a document's lines over as many screens as they need, a day's headlines a sub-page a day, and an article's home with its text, picture, links and where else it appears.
- [Interactive](interactive.md): What viewers take part in: a poll whose votes every viewer sees as they come, kept in a tally the channel holds; a quiz answered with the coloured keys; and sounds to play.
- [Composition](composition.md): Components made of others: a wizard that takes the viewer through a form a step at a time, with Back and Next on the red and green keys.
- [Games](games.md): Games for a page, each viewer playing their own: a roulette wheel that spins down to an article to read, and snake.
- [Common props](common.md): what every control, and every control with a value, takes.
